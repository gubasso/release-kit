# release-kit 0.9.3

- destinations: .pre-commit-config.yaml
- action: landing-write

## What changed

The landed commit-msg hook pins conventional-pre-commit v4.5.0 instead of v4.4.0. The new release also parses a `BREAKING CHANGE` footer of a commit message. The scoped header that the hook requires is unchanged.

## What to do

Run `rk upgrade --apply`. The landing writes the new `rev` into the managed block of `.pre-commit-config.yaml`, and the next commit installs the hook at that revision.
