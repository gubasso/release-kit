# release-kit 0.9.6

- destinations: .pre-commit-config.yaml
- action: operator-step

## What changed

Nothing in the landing changed. This file states a step that the earlier upgrades left out. Under the workflow guards, the managed block of `.pre-commit-config.yaml` carries the `no-commit-to-branch` hook for the trunk at the pre-commit stage, and the `rk-worktree-location` hook. A CI job that runs the hook stages on a trunk checkout runs both, and `no-commit-to-branch` then fails every push to the trunk. A project's trunk CI turned red that way after it took 0.9.5. rk renders no such CI job, because the project owns its CI workflow, so the landing cannot add the skip itself.

## What to do

In every CI job that runs the hook stages on a trunk checkout, set `SKIP=no-commit-to-branch,rk-worktree-location` in the environment of the step that runs `pre-commit`, and append the id of every other check the project classifies as local-only. A job that checks out a detached commit passes `no-commit-to-branch` already, but the skip costs nothing there. Then push to the trunk and make sure that the CI run passes.
